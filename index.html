MONITORING SYSTEM TO KNOW THE PRODUCTION QUANTITY

A digital solution designed to provide full visibility into the number of production outputs in a manufacturing process. This system is capable of automatic and real-time recording, enabling management and operators to quickly determine how many products have been produced, are currently being processed, or are experiencing bottlenecks on the production line.

With this monitoring system, companies no longer rely on time-consuming and error-prone manual reports. Digitally recorded production data is more accurate, easily accessible, and can be analyzed to support more informed decision-making. This is crucial for maintaining consistent quality, evaluating machine and operator performance, and ensuring daily, weekly, and monthly production targets are met.

Additionally, this monitoring system can be integrated with other technologies such as IoT sensors, SCADA, and ERP/MES, creating a interconnected digital ecosystem. As a result, companies can perform end-to-end production control, from raw material input, manufacturing processes, to finished product output. This integration not only enhances operational efficiency but also supports the company's strategy toward digital transformation in the Industry 4.0 era.

Overall, the Monitoring System to Know the Production Quantity is not merely a tool for recording production quantities but also a crucial foundation for improving productivity, reducing downtime, enhancing planning, and strengthening the company's competitiveness in an increasingly competitive market.
